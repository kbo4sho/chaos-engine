# Three-part repairs

Jobs 401–440 have three missing working fittings. Players can run one or two installed parts to find the earliest working handoff. Later benches add spare mounts, extra drawer parts, independent bins and timed receivers.

## Sub-features

- The first six jobs give three different spares and three mounts. Later drawers grow to five spares and four sockets.
- The fitting activity strip reports each installed body's first recorded action. Selecting an action after a test scrubs to the recorded frame. Rewinding before that contact restores Waiting.
- Three-part repairs extend the ordinary shelf, guided route, optional repair coach, comparison film, receiver editor, shared setup, downloadable replay, repair book and paused sandbox import.
- The Cold store crossover combines an ice bed and a bank with sorting tools. Chief mechanic uses seven causal devices and two ordered, parcel-only contacts.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Choose Level map, expand Jump to a tool course and follow Play three-part repairs. Fit one spare and test. After the run choose its activity to inspect the first contact. Return a fitted spare to the drawer using its socket label. Complete the line, then use Watch replay or Copy fitted setup.

Drive puzzle-campaign for all forty new jobs and tool-lessons for the mixed guided route.

## Gotchas

- A tool activation identifies the specific fitted body. An identical fixed station cannot light its activity indicator.
- Cable and drive stations report tension and shaft movement. Their first actions can precede a load striking a physical surface.
- Each timing and direction constraint belongs to its receiver. Two bins can be filled in either order. Numbered targets must be contacted in order.
