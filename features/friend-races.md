# Friend challenges

On any of the 24 work orders, send a completed delivery as a time to beat. The invitation opens an empty machine. A returned challenge opens both measured paths at their first visible separation.

## Sub-features

- Blind start. The challenger’s time and part count appear beside a clean bench. A guest earns a reply by delivering a load.
- Pass the phone. Start a clean invitation on the same device. Prior replay controls and personal record fittings stay tucked away until a fresh delivery. The recent challenges list reopens invitations and returned films.
- Race film. The two deliveries share one physics clock. Each arrival button seeks to its receipt. A finished load holds its final position while the other continues.
- Two-way sharing. Copy challenge link and Copy reply link work across browsers. A selectable link appears when clipboard access is unavailable.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Open Level map and expand Work orders. Complete a delivery and choose Copy challenge link. Open it in a second browser, make a delivery, then choose Copy reply link. Open the returned link to watch the two runs.

## Driving it with verify-chaos-engine

Preconditions: launch and doctor pass with fresh profiles. Run `./scripts/verify-chaos-engine drive friend-races`.

- **Invite.** Deliver each order from the map, copy `#race-copy` and open the address in the guest profile. Verify empty sockets, hidden comparison and zero new records.
- **Reply.** Make a real delivery on the phone. Deny clipboard access and copy the visible fallback. Open the reply in an independent profile, compare both arrival frames, inspect first separation, and reload.
- **Outcomes.** Verify faster, slower and tied replies, rejection of an invalid link and a clean viewer record book. Keep the round-trip addresses and measured receipts.

## Gotchas

An ordinary fitted-setup link reveals its parts immediately. Use the separate challenge and reply controls for a blind race. A viewed replay gives no work-order credit.
