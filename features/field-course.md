# Field stations

Jobs 441–480 introduce eight field tools, then combine them with moving charges, carried equipment, parcel release and timed receivers. The two electric forces act on separate Matter bodies.

## Sub-features

- Pulse adds velocity away from a coil. Swirl adds velocity along the tangent. The approach side and winding decide the direction.
- The lift harness reverses effective gravity. The gyroscope corrects the heading toward the nearest quarter turn. Both can travel with other equipment and be removed at a release hoop.
- A phase gate suspends collisions for 21 fixed steps. The original collision mask and group return when the interval ends. Polarity and field triggers continue to work during the passage.
- Terminals set positive or negative charge. Opposite poles attract and like poles repel. The grounding brush neutralizes a load. Signs distinguish charge without relying on color and arrows show the forces on free bodies.
- The tool lab has eight measured comparisons and sixteen prediction exercises. The field press offers certified changes, including two-tool requests.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Open Level map, expand Jump to a tool course and choose Play field stations. The first machines teach one physical change. In Moving poles, watch the gold marble and the signed blue parcel. Inspect a terminal, brush or phase cue after a run to read the charge and remaining collision interval at that frame.

## Gotchas

- A pole drawn as a small fixed circle supports the reaction force. A signed moving parcel receives a force as well as applying one.
- Phasing changes the collision filter. A phased load still falls, moves and responds to field stations.
- The field display reads the selected film frame. Scrubbing before pickup shows the original state.
